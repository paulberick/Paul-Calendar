Rule 1



The Solar Year begins on the Eastern calendar date

of the Spring Equinox.



\-------------------------



Rule 2



The year contains



364 days



followed by



1 or 2 Festdays.



Festdays belong to the previous year.



\-------------------------



Rule 3



The Lunar Year begins on the first Full Moon

whose Eastern calendar date is equal to

or later than the Spring Equinox date.



\-------------------------



Rule 4



The Full Moon occurring on the Equinox date

is Renewal Moon.



\-------------------------



Rule 5



Moon names advance sequentially.

it simply doesn't exist that year.
